Methodology

Monte Carlo Simulation

100,000+ simulated paths per asset using Geometric Brownian Motion with fat-tailed jump diffusion. Parameters calibrated to trailing 5- and 10-year realized volatility and mean returns.

Drawdown Detection

Peak-to-trough calculation on monthly frequency across each simulation path. A "crash" event is flagged whenever cumulative decline from prior all-time high exceeds the threshold (30%, 50%, 70%).

Recovery Probability

Measured as probability that a path that experiences a crash also achieves a new all-time high within the 10-year simulation window. Low values (3–5%) are an artifact of time constraints, not pessimism about recovery capacity.

Model Limitations

  • GBM assumes log-normal returns — real markets exhibit fat tails and autocorrelation
  • Parameters are estimated from history; regime changes can invalidate assumptions
  • No modeling of correlation regime shifts between assets during crises
  • Bitcoin history is short (15 years) — parameter estimates have high uncertainty
  • Macro events (war, pandemic, regulatory restructuring) modeled implicitly via volatility, not explicitly

Strategic Implications

Position Sizing

  • QQQ: Given 80% crash probability, avoid concentrated single-entry. Dollar-cost averaging reduces sequence-of-returns risk.
  • Bitcoin: High expected return justifies allocation, but position size must reflect maximum tolerable drawdown (assume −80% possible at any time).
  • Rule of thumb: Size Bitcoin so a −80% drawdown equals 1–2% of total portfolio loss.

Time Horizon Alignment

  • Short-term (<3yr): QQQ and BTC both face elevated drawdown risk in current valuation environment. Cash or short-duration bonds offer optionality.
  • Medium-term (3–7yr): Halving tailwinds, AI monetization cycle, and institutional adoption all peak in this window.
  • Long-term (>7yr): Strong positive expected value for both assets. Maximum patience has historically been rewarded.

Downside Protection

  • QQQ hedges: Protective puts (costly but precise), inverse ETFs (tracking error risk), sector rotation into defensives.
  • Bitcoin hedges: Covered calls to generate income during sideways periods; cash position to deploy on drawdowns (>50%).
  • Portfolio level: Gold, TIPS, and trend-following managed futures historically provide crisis alpha.

Actionable Framework for QQQ

  1. Accept ~80% probability of a 30%+ drawdown as the base case — plan for it, don't hope against it.
  2. DCA monthly regardless of price level; never go all-in at all-time highs.
  3. Maintain 12–18 months of living expenses outside equities to avoid forced selling during drawdowns.
  4. Rebalance back to target allocation after 30%+ crashes — this is the historically optimal response.
  5. Monitor AI capex-to-revenue ratios as leading indicator of valuation sustainability.

Actionable Framework for Bitcoin

  1. Determine maximum psychologically tolerable drawdown before establishing any position.
  2. Size position so that a −80% decline is financially and emotionally survivable.
  3. Use halving cycles as a secular framework — accumulate in the 6 months post-halving.
  4. Never leverage Bitcoin — volatility destroys leveraged positions during normal bear markets.
  5. Self-custody significant holdings (>$50K equivalent) to eliminate exchange counterparty risk.

  • TSLA — Never sell regardless of price. Write covered calls but never allow assignment below $500. The thesis is Optimus, Robotaxi, energy, and AI compounding for decades.
  • GOOGL — Never sell. Google is the AI infrastructure play, search moat, and cloud. Hold forever.
  • NVDA — Never sell. GPU monopoly for AI training and inference. Position to grow significantly.
  • AMZN — Never sell. AWS + logistics moat + advertising flywheel.
  • XPEV — Never sell until $50+. Tesla of China with humanoid and autonomous thesis. Avg cost $24.95.

Concentration Rules

  • TSLA single-position cap: watch at 20%, review at 25%. Currently 17.7% — in range.
  • No single non-TSLA position to exceed 15% of portfolio.
  • QQQI income position: no hard cap. Target 10–15% of portfolio.
  • Bitcoin (IBIT+BTCI+MSTR combined): maintain below 8% of portfolio.

Rebalancing Triggers

  • Add to NVDA on any dip below $180 using IRA cash or settled proceeds.
  • Add QQQI shares quarterly from covered call income (manual DRIP).
  • Nuclear bucket: hold all positions. Add NNE/OKLO on 20%+ dips.
  • XPEV: add at $12 or below (cost averaging). Never sell above $20 unless thesis breaks.
  • Dead positions (LCID, TNYA, AEXA): harvest tax losses when advantageous. Do not add.

Tax Rules

  • Bluestone $50K tax reserve is locked — do not touch for any equity purchase.
  • All IRA trades are tax-free. Prioritize rebalancing and loss harvesting inside IRA.
  • CMA taxable accounts: be mindful of short-term vs long-term gains. Hold >1 year where possible.
  • Year 5 pivot (QQQ+VOO → QQQI rotation in CMA-28Z) will trigger ~$31K tax. Fund from AMDA income.
